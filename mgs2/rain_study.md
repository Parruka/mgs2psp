# The game-side rain study (task #164, 2026-11-03)

What the game's own rain work costs on the deck, whether it can be cut the game's own way, and what that changes. Options G1 / G2 of `deck_costs.md` §4.3, and G0: skip the game's work for the rain's four sprite actors, which gl17 no longer draws (Ffm's card, 18:19); §10 does the same for rain.c's streaks, which gl17 no longer draws either (Ffm's card, 17:38). Tags: [C] checked, [I] inferred.

## 1. The rain's actors and what they share [C]

- **The actors** (each names itself by file name at +0x28): rain.c (the streaks), line_sph.c, body_sph.c, charaspread.c and rain_gas_pers.c (the four sprite owners gl17 leaves out of the picture, `stagec_notes.md` 4.13). Their acts in w00a: rain 0x31af28, line_sph 0x333380, body_sph 0x3271a8, charaspread 0x2d30c8 (its draws in 0x2d2858), rain_gas_pers 0x331160 (GV_SetNamedActor 0x13f378: act, die, name).
- **All five draw from the stage's random seed** (w00a: the word at 0x1fc954, x = x * 0x5d588b65 + 1). Seed sites in each act: rain 1, line_sph 1 (four draws a vertex, speed_notes §341), body_sph 2, charaspread 1, rain_gas_pers 3.
- **Each stage image has its own seed** with the same generator: d00t 0x1ffa54, d01t 0x1ff554, w00a 0x1fc954, w01a 0x1fbd74 (1 in each image file). All of a stage's random users share it (in w00a, KR_RandU / KR_RandS at 0x233498 / 0x2334f0; 345 seed sites in w00a). The main program has no generator of its own (none of the usual LCG constants in 0x100000-0x17cf78).
- **Where they run** (the census of 4.13): body_sph, line_sph and rain_gas_pers from the opening (vb 8,756) to the end of the deck; charaspread from vb 23,261 to 32,197, the last part inside (about 10 s after the door).

**Why kC still changes the game inside** (EE dumps of the reference and kC at vb 31,700, just inside):
1. **The game clock.** The twin's game clock (and the PSP harness's: the state hashes are the same on both) advances by 64 EE cycles each loop iteration of the game's code (`runtime/kernel.h` K_POLL_CYCLES; the draw pipeline takes none, by Ffm's OK). Without the four, two heavy frames on the deck end a vblank sooner: vb 24,009-24,012 and 24,300-24,310, where the reference takes three vblanks a frame and kC two. By vb 31,700 kC has made one frame more (14,615 against 14,614), so the game's steps meet the recorded pad one step out of phase from then on. The sound state is equal again from vb 31,300 to about 36,500, then differs (a guard situation inside, about 1 min after the door), and at vb 37,431 the new code runs.
2. **The random numbers.** On the deck the seed sequence changes as soon as one of them is skipped. They do not carry into the next stage: w01a's seed is equal in both at vb 31,700.
3. **Memory addresses.** After the first destroy, later allocations sit 16 bytes lower: at vb 31,700, 986 words of the program's data differ, almost all pointers off by 0x10, plus the frame's buffer half (the extra frame). Not shown to change behaviour by itself [I].

**So switching them off is not invisible:** the picture is the same as gl17's (it leaves them out already), but the game plays differently from the deck on. On our test route that means a hit inside; in free play nobody could tell which way the dice fell [I]. G1 and G2 (rain off or thinner) work the same way: fewer draws and fewer loop iterations, so a different game after the first changed frame.

## 3. What an invisible skip (G0) would need [I, from the code and the 2026-10-19 trace]

G0 is the roadmap's E4 (render-only actors skip the unshown step, speed_notes §15 and §22) done on every step for these four, since gl17 never draws them. Each of the four replaced by a stub that:
- keeps the actor alive (no destroy, so every allocation stays where it is);
- advances the seed by exactly the draws its act would make (an LCG jumps ahead n draws in a few multiplications);
- polls the game clock exactly as its act would (one poll a loop iteration), as the native line_sph already does (speed_notes §30: its poll budget is the copies' iterations, the vertex loop's and the line loop's);
- leaves exactly what other code reads from it. The x86 trace of 2026-10-19 (`prof/act_readers_vb24001_24100.txt`, the deck, vb 24,001-24,100: words a step that another reader takes from each actor's writes) gives line_sph 6 (VIF0 DMA 4, ripple 1, chim_smo 1), body_sph 6 (fade_lgt 5), charaspread 8, rain_gas_pers 40 (rain_cm 7, bgmfader 7, camera 4, camerad 4, ...): the seed, the shared work area 0x1b4700-0x1b48ff and the scratchpad's leftovers [I] (speed_notes §15). Everything else they write goes only to the draw pipeline.

**The proof would be the masked game-state hash** (boot.c `MGS2_GS_MASK`: EE RAM without the memory only the renderer reads, as for the reduced pipeline, speed_notes §33-34) equal to the original's along the whole route on x86. The picture stays gl17's (checkable frame by frame on the PC); the full state hashes change only where the renderer alone reads, so the PSP's references would be the G0 build's own.

What each one needs:
- **line_sph** [C, speed_notes §30]: stateless; draws = 4 a vertex, vertices = word 0 of each line << 5, none while hidden (the global word at 0x1ed438 or the actor's +84). The stub can skip all of the vertex work.
- **body_sph** [C, the code at 0x3271a8]: one draw a step for a random tilt of the rain's direction; then for each body part (actor +116 parts) its vertices and normals go to the scratchpad, the normals are turned by the part's matrix, and every vertex whose normal faces the rain (dot product above 0.7) gets a drop: its position turned, three draws jitter it by up to 80 units each way, at most 16 drops a part. The draw count depends on the bodies' poses, so the stub must still turn every normal and test it: about half of the act stays [I]. Created by the characters' code (Snake's at 0x292da0, the guards' at 0x261490), one per body.
- **charaspread** [first reading of 0x2d2858]: a particle system (a life counter a particle; a dead one is born again with a draw, under conditions not read in full yet), so the stub keeps the particles' lives and births and skips their placing and drawing.
- **rain_gas_pers**: draws inside three per-particle loops (0x331630-0x331810, 0x331890-0x331ad8, 0x331b98-0x331c30); not read yet. Its 40 words a step read by the camera and the music fader need a look first.

## 6. G0 for line_sph, built and proven on the PC [C, x86] (task #165, 2026-11-03; Ffm chose "Exact skip" on the card, 22:03)

**Design** (private tree `/home/claude/sbg0`, `nat/native_w00a.c`; `runtime/host_time.h` OPT2_G0 2048 and OPT2_G0H 4096, off by default; gl16's and gl17's cycle words have both bits set, so a build that takes them must choose its words). In a G0 prim's stretch (0x3334e0-0x333774) A and B stay as they are in RAM. The seed jumps the prim's 4 c draws at once (a table of the generator's maps for 4 k draws, k = 0..256); the last vertex is placed for the float registers the loop leaves (F0-F3); the last copy's registers are B's with h at +6; polls, time, LO / HI and the stack words as the translation. The scratchpad image the copies leave is placed at the flush from the prim's seed (4 draws a vertex, w from A) and from B with h at +6. **G0H** also sets the prim's hide bits (+164 |= 0x3000, the bits the act's own hidden path sets), so the draw pipeline builds nothing from the stale vertices; the act's next turn ORs them (hidden, 0x333400) or ANDs them off (visible, 0x333434) before any use. Tools and how to rebuild: `tools/dev/rain/g0/README.md`.

**Per-prim check** (`-DNAT_CHECK`, x86, the indoor route to vb 37,800): after each G0 stretch the translation runs from the same state and both results are compared: registers, LO / HI, time, seed, the 16 KB scratchpad, B except its halfwords at +6, A's w words, +164 equal to the translation's with 0x3000 (all 32 MB every 64th check). **201,884 prims, all G0 with hiding, 0 different** (3,155 with all RAM). The stretch and the copy routines it calls (0x33bd38, 0x33bd58, 0x3b7c88, 0x3b7d68) hold no VU0 instruction, so VU0 is untouched either way.

**Whole route** (gl17's settings, x86 twin, G0H against G0 off): the virtual time, the sound state and all 17,664 picture digests equal at every point. The RAM hash differs from vb 23,300, and so does the masked game-state hash that §3 planned as the proof (96 of 378 points): besides line_sph's own words, the pipeline builds shorter lists, and not all of its memory is in the mask (`prof/gs_mask_v1.txt`: the memory only the renderer read in v19's trace run). EE dumps at vb 23,500 and 33,500 (`rundump.sh`, compared word by word) show 328 and 3,665 differing words outside the mask and the added ranges. At 23,500: 316 in the scratchpad, VU1 DMA lists being built (the tags' addresses, VIF unpack and program-call codes, vectors), and 12 pointers into the lists at 0x1b8bb0-0x1b900c. At 33,500: 3,658 in the packet buffers at 0x1600000-0x162ffff and 0x16b0000-0x16effff [I], 3,486 of them holding data in the G0-off run and zero in the G0H run (packets G0H never built [I]), and 7 pointers into them. ## 7. H3: the other three actors' prims hidden, built and proven on the PC [C, x86] (task #165, 2026-11-03)

**Design** (the same private tree; the w00a unit `wpl/gen_s_w00a.h` is v31's translation with six stores changed (and, for the read-hash build, the 14 loads below marked); `OPT2_H3` = 8192 in `runtime/host_time.h`, off by default and set in gl16's and gl17's cycle words, like G0 and G0H). Each of the three acts shows its prims by loading their +164 word, ANDing the hide bits (0x3000) off and storing it back: body_sph at 0x327284, 0x327310 and 0x327404, charaspread at 0x2d31cc and 0x2d31dc (its two prims), rain_gas_pers at 0x33127c. With H3 these six stores write the word with the hide bits set, as the acts' own hide paths do, so the draw pipeline builds nothing for the prims. The registers keep the translation's values and no other store changes. The acts' own loads of the word are 14 read-modify-writes that OR the bits on or AND them off before any use, so what they compute is the translation's. The acts still do all their own work (G0 for that comes next). Diffs and tools: `tools/dev/rain/g0/README.md` "H3".

**Whole route** (gl17's settings, x86 twin `/home/claude/sbg0/x3`, the indoor route to vb 37,800, all off `ffffc1df,ffffc7df` against G0H with H3 `fffff9df,ffffffdf`): the virtual time, the sound state and all 17,664 picture digests (lists and frames) equal at every point. With H3 off this build gives §6's G0-off run exactly (the RAM hash equal at all 378 points). The masked game-state hash differs at the same 96 points as with G0H alone (§6), so H3 adds none.

**What the game reads** (the read-hash build with H3, `/home/claude/sbg0/xt3`; boot.c marks the word at each H3 store, any later store unmarks it, and a load of a marked word at one of the 14 read-modify-write sites is hashed with the two bits clear, as the translation stored it; a load of a marked word anywhere else is reported). All off against G0H with H3, the indoor route to vb 37,800:
- **the same number of loads in all 37,799 vblanks**, and no load of a hidden word except at the 14 sites (no report);
- first pass, every load hashed: **the loads differ in the same 2,473 vblanks as with G0H alone** (§6: 23,260-23,470, the 12 first frames inside, 29,069-31,317 and 31,660). 13 of them, from 23,260 to 31,660, were logged load by load: one load a frame differs, always one of §6's two clip-flag reloads (rain_gas_pers in 12, w01a's P313e60 at 31,660). - with the two clip-flag reloads of §6 left out of the hash, **the loads differ in 53 vblanks**: the same 12 first frames inside as with G0H alone (23,664-23,688) and 41 on the aft deck (30,719-30,808). **All 53 were logged load by load** (`h3log.sh`, five batches): every load at the same address, and every differing byte one of §6's leftovers.
