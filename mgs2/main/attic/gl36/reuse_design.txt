2. WHAT THE PROBE SAYS
a. THE PLAN'S LEVER AS WRITTEN HAS A HIT RATE OF 0 [C]. The matrix (T + 0 .. 3, the constant block's first four quadwords, which
   holds the camera) is different in every picture for every group; nothing else differs in the rain. A drawn group's GE output
   (its GerXform: world, projection, viewport) depends on the matrix, and so does its cut test and its depth-range note. So no
   drawn group's GE words are ever equal to the last picture's, and no group's translation can be replayed whole with words copied:
   the plan's target (-50% of 60.8 us a group in the rain wait ME turns) cannot be reached by copying words. [C]
b. What does repeat [C]: everything that is not camera-dependent. For drawn groups the vertices, strips, indices and batch state
   are equal to the last picture's in every case seen; the cut outcome is equal in 99.9%; the runs of groups come in the same order
   in 96% of the rain wait's groups (two runs of ~285 groups a picture); the GS registers and the VU buffers are the same in 97-100%.
c. What a group's translation reads and writes (t35's fast path, xl_p04_fast + gl_call/xc_call + gf_drew) [C, code], and what

3. WHAT EACH REUSE CAN BE WORTH (upper bounds, before the reuse path's own cost) [I, from the PSP stage times above]
  R0 whole group, words copied (the plan's form): hit rate 0 [C] -> 0.
  R1 the VU memory model's work of a run (R-a below): the register stage's copies of the block's rest and the material and the three
     descriptors, for every group whose writes a later group of the same run overwrites: about 5-6 of the 9.8 us register stage
     [I: the stage's code by lines; the header, the matrix and the tag template stay], plus the transfer-end decode of ~850
     descriptors a run (xl_flush -> vm_all) that then has a few: about 0.5-1 us a group [I: the descriptor loop's length].
  R2 the box for cut groups (R-b below): the positions stage (4.3 us) for the 54% of groups that the cut then leaves out: about
     2.3 us a group on average [I].
  R3 the cut groups' converter set-up (host call + GS state, way back): a further ~3-5 us on cut groups [I]; needs the call's
     wrappers (twin and PSP differ) and gl_call's state reproduced outside the converter: NOT in gl36 (risk against gain).
  R4 the GS model's effects replayed per run (the CLUT loads, the texture look-ups, the drawn areas): these are ordered effects
     whose inputs (the CLUT state, the cache's LRU, the GS model's counts that gl_tex's memo reads) change from group to group
     inside the run; gl35 measured that a guarded replay of one of them (the texture, K3) costs MORE than the step on the PSP
     (+0.76 us a group [C PSP]) and the drawn areas replay (K1) saves 1.6 [C PSP], which gl36 keeps. NOT in gl36.
  So the most the reuse can reach in gl36's form is about R1 + R2 = 8-9 us of 60.8 a group (about -14%) [I], net perhaps -6 to -8
  us after the guards and the record pictures [I], against the plan's -30 us. THE PLAN'S TARGET (-50%) IS NOT REACHABLE [C probe
  for the cause, I for the size]. In the model's terms the parts touched are about 8% of the ship's native work a picture in the
  rain wait ME turn (xl_p04_fast's copies and descriptors, vc_df0_pos_k for cut groups: model_split.out parts 6 and 12) [C model
  shares, I for the split inside the functions].
  The design below builds R1 + R2 (the best part that is exact by construction and needs no GS-model replay), behind its own switch,
  in turns, so the PSP's split timers measure it stage by stage.

4. THE DESIGN (keys MGS2_NAT_REUSE=<0/1> for the whole run, MGS2_NAT_REUSE_CYCLE=a,b,c,d by MGS2_OPT_CYCLE's turns as the other
   MGS2_NAT_* keys; MGS2_NAT_RCHK=1 the check mode; fast native groups only, so it needs MGS2_NAT_FAST in that turn)
4.1 Units and where the decision is made
  A RUN is the set of consecutive native groups the walk hands to the translator in one go (ge/xlme.c lw_nat on the PSP's walks,
  boot/nat_x86.c nat_walk on the twin): nothing else reaches the translator between two groups of a run. The groups are an array
  per packet buffer (boot/nat_stub.h g_nat); a group's place in it (its index) is the same from picture to picture when the picture
  repeats. The switch is taken per pipeline call (NatBuf.ru, set with the array's start), so every group of a picture has the same.
  The kept table (host memory, NR entries of 64 bytes, one per group index; the PSP: a block outside newlib's heap, 147 KB for 2,304
  groups, fewer entries if the block is refused, none = the switch does nothing): per index the record's words that the
  translation reads (material, streams, counts, flags' low bits, the header's four words, MSCAL: 40 bytes, compared whole), the box,
  the first triangle without ADC (i1), bits (in the S set; box valid), and at a run's first index the run's length and the VU
  buffers (BASE, OFFSET, DBF).
4.2 The test (before the run is fed)
  The run [i0, i0 + n) is REPLAYED when: the switch is on for its picture; the table has entries for all of it; the entry at i0
  holds a recorded run of length n with BASE, OFFSET and DBF equal to now; every group's record words equal its entry's (a full
  compare, no hash); every group is a fast group. Otherwise the run is RECORDED (fed as today, with the record kept, section 4.4).
  The test costs one pass over the run's records (10 words a group compared) [C, code].
4.3 What a replayed run does differently (everything else is today's path, in today's order)
  R-a, the front end (ge/xlate.c xl_p04_fast): a group NOT in the run's S set skips the VU memory writes that a later group of the
  same run overwrites: the constant block's quadwords 4 .. 0x13 and 0x16 .. 0x17 (q0 groups still copy 0 .. 3, the matrix, and
  0x15, the tag template, which the call reads), the material's 8 quadwords at T + 0xe8, and the three streams' descriptors and
  own[] marks. It still does: the form tests (a group that does not fit takes xl_p04_core in full, as today), every counter (the
  same increments: the translator's St, the census, xl_nf_n, the lane counts), the RC row, the header, the nf memo, V.tops / DBF,
  the A+D packet (the GS registers, the CLUT loads), the tag's PRIM, the call with the same XlRec (the streams' pointers), the
  drawn areas. A group IN the S set runs exactly today's path.
  The S set (recorded): the groups that are the LAST writer, within the run, of at least one quadword of those kinds (block rest,
  material, stream data) in either VU buffer. Exactness of the VU memory model: every such quadword's final value and owner is
  its last writer's, which is in S and writes as today; a quadword skipped by a group outside S is written again later in the run
  by S or by another group, in order, so after the run (and at every read inside it, see below) the memory as a read sees it is
  today's [C, argument; checked: section 5]. Reads inside a run: the converter of a fast group reads T + 0 .. 3, T + 0x14 and abs
  2 .. 5 (written in both forms) and the tag template T + 0x15 (written in both forms by q0 groups); nothing reads the skipped
  quadwords between their skipped write and their next write [C, code: xlate.c call path, xconv.c xc_call for XlRec.nf]. What
  differs is only the representation: fewer pending descriptors (ud_n, ud_vecs), so the transfer's end decodes less, and a full
  table comes later (a later group is then taken by the fast path where today it would go to xl_p04_core: the same state and
  output, gl34's proof [C, attic/gl34]).
  R-b, the converter (ge/xconv.c xc_call, XlRec.nf with a kept box): the transform is taken as today (vc_df0_pos_k's memo and
  counters) but the positions' loop is not run: Dn is the header's count, the box is the kept one; then the cut test as today. A
  group the cut leaves out takes today's cut path, its depth-range note with i1 kept (no read of stream B) and, when the box's
  bound does not decide (xc_df0_zin), the positions computed then; a group the cut keeps runs the positions' loop then and goes
  on as today. The box and i1 are functions of the static streams only (stream A's positions; stream B's ADC flags), whose
  identity (address and count) is in the compared record [C probe: 0 of 609,235 groups had other stream bytes for the same
  identity in the picture before].
4.4 Recording (a run that is not replayed, the switch on): fed as today with, in xl_p04_fast, the last writer of each quadword of
  those kinds noted (a uint16 per VU quadword, cleared at the run's end), and in xc_call the box and i1 kept for the group's index
  when vc_df0_pos_k gave a count; at the run's end S is made from the notes, and the entries (record words, box, i1, S bit, the
  run's length and VU buffers) are written. A run with a group that did not take the fast path is not kept.
4.5 The check mode (MGS2_NAT_RCHK=1): a run that would be replayed is fed in full and recorded again, and compared with the kept
  entries: the S set, every box and i1, the run's length: "differing" counted. The twin also compares, frame by frame, the arms
  with the switch on and off: the list and frame digests, the counts, the dz text, the content, natb / natz / natq, and the
  translator's state (nats: the VU memory as a read sees it, the GS registers, the front end, the VIF and GIF state, the counters).
4.6 What stays as gl35 delivered it, and the base: MGS2_NAT_FAST=1 and MGS2_NAT_KEEP=1 (the drawn-areas keep only) in every turn;
  the keep bits (xl_keep_bits) reset at each picture's start (gl_begin), so a turn without keeps has none (gl35's oddity).
4.7 Memory: the table, 64 B a group: 147 KB for 2,304 groups on the PSP (a block of the system's memory outside newlib's heap, as
  the groups' arrays; gl35's PSP run had about 2.6 MB free at its end [C PSP]); if refused, 1,536 / 1,024 / 512 entries are tried;
  groups past the table's end are recorded and replayed only when their whole run fits. The last-writer notes: 2 KB of static data.
  The twin: calloc.

5. GATES FOR THE LEVER (in addition to gl35's)
  - twin: switch on in every turn, off, by turns in both orders, with the check mode: 685/685 hash lines each; all 17,664 frames equal
    in every column between them; the check mode's differing 0 (S sets, boxes, i1);
  - emulator: the delivered cfg 62/62, 916/916 digests equal to the twin; the check mode's differing 0; pictures against gl35's;
  - counts in every 'nat reuse' line: runs replayed / recorded, groups replayed, groups in S, boxes used, cut by a kept box.

6. EXPECTED [I unless marked]
  - hit rate: 96% of the rain wait's fast groups in runs that repeat [C probe]; the share in S UNKNOWN until built (expected a few
    groups a run: the last q0 group and the last group of each buffer, and the groups owning stream tails).
  - per group in the rain wait's ME turns: register stage -4..-6 us, positions -2.3 us on average, the transfer-end decode -0.5..-1
    us: about -7..-9 us of 60.8 (-12..-15%) on the PSP's timers if the model's per-stage shares hold [I]; the model run will give
    [C model] figures; with the ~4x overrating seen before, the PSP step time may show 1-2 ms a step [I].
  - step time: the ME is the rain's limit (85-88 ms a step, ~4 idle); a saving there shows up until the main CPU's turn work
    (logic 43 ms a step + waits) becomes the limit [I].
  - the plan's -50% target: NOT reachable by this form (section 3).
