# DEVIATIONS.md entry draft for V44 (gl44): for the lead to place; NOT in DEVIATIONS.md

Suggested place: section E5 ("Exact skips (not deviations)"), as one more bullet beside G0H, H3, NBS, G0C, H3R and G0R, since its proof is E5's:
every load the game makes is at the same address in every vblank, with the same bytes except leftovers no code acts on, and the virtual
time, the sound state and every picture are equal. Text:

- **V44: line_sph's hidden prims as a stub, rain.c's hidden streak loops skipped (`OPT2_V44`, MGS2_OPT2 bit 22 = 4194304; t44/boot/native_w00a.{c,h}, gen44/wpl/gen_s_w00a.h's hook at 0x31b47c by attic/gl44_vita/v44_apply.py; design attic/gl44_vita/gl44_vita_design.md, built and gated in attic/gl44).** On only where G0 + G0H (line_sph) and G0R + H3R (rain.c) already hide the prims; with any of them off it steps aside by itself and today's natives or the translation run.
  - **What:**
    - line_sph (0x333380): in a V44 turn every prim of the act is a stub. It draws the prim's random numbers from the stage seed (seed = A[c] x seed + C[c], the translation's count), adds the prim's game time (its interrupt polls x 64), sets the hide bits (+164 |= 0x3000) and, on the act's last unit, places the first 32 vertices of the unit's scratchpad image (0x70000000 on): a later act, putmodel.c, reads vertices 16..21 of it without writing them (an uninitialised read in the game itself; 22 is the measured minimum). Before the stub, prims of the act still pending for the G0 form are placed first (review change; counted, 0 on the route). The vertex maths of hidden prims is not done; registers it would leave (R2-R7, F0-F6) and the scratchpad beyond the tail keep older values.
    - rain.c (0x31af28): of each act's 16 entries, the 15 whose first prim set is all hidden skip both vertex loops and the fill loop (0x31b47c -> L31b6c0) and add the loops' game time (their polls x 64) in one go; the 16th runs as before. The loops' VU0 work is not done, so VF registers and VU0's random register keep older values; the skipped entries' vertex buffers keep their old (hidden, never drawn) contents.
  - **Impact [C twin, x86 twin of gl44, route i to vb 37,800; arms: V44 never on (s44n), in its turns as delivered (s44), on in every word (von)]:**
    - **Seed and time.** The seed log is identical in all arms and in gl43's code with V44 never on (off43): every act's seeds and game time at entry (27,764 lines from vb 23,000), every seed change with the act that drew it (98,024, the guards' among them) and the splash acts' times (12,310). The act log's 13,059 scheduler passes have the same acts, seeds and times (md5 882c4e6d6bf9 in all four arms).
    - **Sound and pictures.** IOP and frame-buffer hashes equal at all 685 points; every picture equal: 37,800 live lines, 14,203 digest and 14,203 content lines.
    - **Off is gl43.** With the bit never set the twin is gl43's: hashref = hashref_gl43 byte for byte (685 lines), digests and content = gl43's arm, seed log equal over gl43's range.
    - **Read hash.** Every load outside the draw pipeline, hashed per vblank (rh44.sh, natives on in the trace build): read counts equal in all 37,799 vblanks; hashes equal but in 188 vblanks of the walk (s44, 30,925-31,227) and 160 (von, 30,903-31,271), and equal again from there to 37,800. Logged load by load in 24 of them (the first, middle and last vblank of every differing range of both pairs; 2.77 M + 0.91 M loads per arm): 592 + 96 (s44) and 480 + 160 (von) loads differ, the address never. Every differing load is the w lane of a quadword: S_w00a_P3420e8 stores VF8-VF11 (0x3423c4-d0, 0x3424d0-dc) after giving VF9-VF11 only x, y, z, so their w is whatever earlier VU0 code left (4 after rain.c's loops, 0 when V44 skipped them). Those quadwords are read only by the copies S_w00a_P3b7d68 / P3b7c88 / P342608, which carry w as it is, and by DG_RotVector (P125f78), which reads x, y and z only: no load sees a different x, y or z, and the path never changes.
    - **State hash.** The EE and GS state hashes differ from vb 29,300 (the end of the first V44 turn, t52) by design: only rain.c's own hidden vertex buffers (15 stale entries x 2 KB, and 2 x 1.5 KB of the 16th, which gets other vertices because VU0's random register has advanced less; RAM dumps in the design's s5.3). After the deck they are freed blocks nothing reuses, so the hash stays different to the end of the route. gl44's hashref is its own (equal to gl43's to vb 29,200).
    - **Counters.** von: line_sph 201,884 prims stubbed, 0 scratchpad flushes (V44 off: 19,696), rain.c 36,930 entries skipped (15 of 16 in each of 2,462 acts); 0 last units shorter than the tail. The schedule's 11 V44 turns: 8,200 prims and 1,500 entries a full turn.
  - **Impact [C emu]:** the PSP build on the emulator (loop apart): 62/62 hashes = gl44's hashref; content of the 61 frames of the two V44 digest turns (t55, t73) = the twin's; the V44 counters of every turn = the twin's; XP check 114,008 plans, 0 differing; heap unchanged (226 KB at vb 536 without PGO, as block A's build).
  - **Cost [C model, gl44's final build, ms a PSP step; main = the main CPU]:** K-N2BV - K-N2B (the native path, the delivery's kind): rain wait 6 pairs -1.02 to -1.56, mean -1.32 main (game -0.88, ME about 0); walk 4 pairs mean -5.29 (-1.21 to -8.18; the walk's own K-N2B-to-K-N2B spread is +2.39 / -4.78, so noisy); all 10 -2.91 (ME -2.11).  K-BV - K-B mean -0.13 (K-B's own spread -3.98: no reading).  K7BV - K7B mean -0.42 (the stub's own logic).  Expected on the PSP [I]: about -1.1 ms a step in the rain wait (0.87 x the model), about 1.6 % of gl43's K-N2B main 69.3.  The PSP run of gl44 measures it (README (a)-(c)).  The no-PGO build gave -1.43 (game -0.95).
  - **Not done:** a NAT_CHECK compare (its per-call check against the translation would flag the stub by design: the dropped leftovers are the difference; the seed and time log and the read hash are the proof instead).
  - **What you see:** nothing changes on screen: the prims it leaves are hidden and never drawn.
  - **Undo:** clear bit 22 of MGS2_OPT2 and the MGS2_OPT2_CYCLE words. NOTE: gl43's words (ffdff9ff, fffff9ff) carry bit 22: with gl44's EBOOT they turn V44 on.
  - **Status:** gl44 = a measurement build (V44 in t52, t53, t55, t58, t60, t62, t65, t66, t69, t71, t73). Not on by default (OPT2_DEFAULT leaves it out).
