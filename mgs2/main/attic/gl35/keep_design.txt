gl35 KEEP DESIGN (written before the code; 2026-11-21 17:50 UTC; build thread)
Marks: [C] measured or read in code, [C model] cost model, [I] inferred (basis named), UNKNOWN.

1. WHAT "KEEP" CAN MEAN HERE, FROM A PROBE ON THE TWIN [C]
t35 = t34 with counters only, run to vblank 31,400 with the fast front end in every turn (build_gl35/tw/x86/out_probe.log,
out_probe2.log):
  fast native groups                                          872,659
  ... material packet byte-equal to the group before           144,343 (16.5 percent)
  ... no GS traffic of the translator since the group before   866,954 (99.3 percent)
  ... with a constant block sent (q0)                          269,242 (30.9 percent)
  texture look-ups past the one-entry memo (all callers)     3,422,323
  ... same TEX0 and CLAMP seen earlier in the same picture   2,709,562 (79 percent; 64-slot table, 537,638 slot conflicts)
The ship's material packet is 7 quadwords: CLAMP_1, TEX2_1 (CLD 4: load the CLUT if CBP differs from CBP0), TEX0_1 (CLD 0),
ALPHA_1, register 7f (no-op), and two data quadwords written to PRIM; then PRIM is set from the tag template [C, sampled].
Consequences:
  - "the same as the group before" covers the material in only one group of six: each material has its own CLUT (CBP), so a
    real CLUT load and a texture look-up with new inputs happen at five groups of six. A keep by "equal to the last one" cannot
    reach the texture or the CLUT.
  - what does stay equal from group to group in 99.3 percent: every register the material packet does not write (FRAME, ZBUF,
    SCISSOR, TEST, PRMODE...), i.e. everything the drawn-areas bookkeeping reads.
  - the texture a material asks for was asked for earlier in the same picture in 79 percent of look-ups: a keep by "this
    material's answer of this picture" does reach the texture.

2. THE RULE FOR EVERY PART
A kept step is a REPLAY: it leaves every byte of state the full step would leave (counters, stamps, LRU links included), so the
translator's, the GS model's and the texture cache's state are equal after EVERY group, not only where they are read. So no
reader needs a rule of its own, the GE words cannot differ, and every log counter stays equal. A replay is taken only when
tokens show that nothing the full step reads has changed since the full step that was recorded; otherwise the full step runs
(and records). Nothing in gl35 is "pixel-identical but not word-identical".

3. THE PARTS (own key MGS2_NAT_KEEP=<bits>, MGS2_NAT_KEEP_CYCLE=<bits>,<bits>,... by the turns; fast groups only)

K1, bit 1: drawn areas (PSP: 4.6 us a group [C]; model: gu_drawn 2.97 ms a picture + gf_drew [C model])
  Full step: gf_drew(F, dctx) -> for each context in dctx: gu_drawn(frame area) and, with depth writes on, gu_drawn(depth area).
  For the ship both calls take gu_drawn's first return ("one of the last two calls again, area not written since").
  That return's effects [C, gsup2.c gu_drawn]: settle(m) (nothing to do unless a transfer is waiting to go in), st.drawn_kept++,
  the area's last = ++dt_clock, the development counters. Nothing else.
  Reads it relies on:
    a. its arguments: F.gr[0x40+c], [0x4c+c], [0x4e+c], [0x47+c], and dctx from PRIM / PRMODE / PRMODECONT (0x00, 0x1b, 0x1a);
    b. in the GS model: the two last-calls records dl[0..1], the area's dirty flag, and settle's condition (cur, r[cur].in).
  Tokens:
    a. xl_gs_gen: moves at every GS traffic of the translator that is not a kept fast group's own (gif_qw, image_run, call(),
       xl_reset) AND when a fast group's own packet writes a register outside the set {06 07 08 09 14 15 16 17 42 43 7f, 00};
       dctx is computed for real at every group and compared with the recorded one.
    b. GuMap.dgen (new): moves in gu_drawn whenever it goes past the kept return, in commit(), where an area is marked dirty,
       at gu_trxdir / gu_image / gu_image_run, at init. While it stands still, dl[], every dirty flag and settle's condition
       are as they were after the recorded step (which had settled).
  Record: after a full gf_drew of a fast group in which every gu_drawn call took the kept return (GuMap counts calls and kept
  returns since a mark and lists the areas, at most 2): the areas in order, dctx, both tokens.
  Replay: per area, in order: st.drawn_kept++, last = ++dt_clock, the development counters (gu_drawn_again).
  Check (MGS2_NAT_KCHK=1): the full step runs anyway; counted "differing" if its calls did not all take the kept return, or
  not on the recorded areas in the recorded order, or dgen moved.

K2, bit 2: the A+D packet (PSP: 6.0 us a group [C]; model: gf_reg 2.42 ms a picture, gu_clut_load 3.24 [C model])
  Not a replay but the same statements with the calls taken out where the call can do nothing:
    - PRIM (register 0, two data quadwords and the tag): gf_reg's case 0 and gs_write's store written in line;
    - TEX0 (06 / 07): gu_clut_load is called only when it could do something: the texture format is indexed and CLD is 1..5
      (gu_clut_load returns at once otherwise [C]). TEX2's call stays (five groups of six load a CLUT for real).
  Reads it relies on: none beyond the full step's own. Check: plain = keep on the twin (every counter, every frame).

K3, bit 4 (native fast groups' look-ups) and bit 8 (every look-up, for the people in gl36): the texture
  (PSP: 8.2 us a group [C]; model: gl_tex 1.89 + tk_get_um 3.59 ms a picture [C model])
  Full step for a look-up past gl_tex's one-entry memo: tk_get_um (front hit -> um_hit -> gu_clut_id -> tk_pal by hint or
  find) -> the picture's texture table (add_tex: found) -> memo. On that "pure" path its effects are [C, texcache.c, gelive.c]:
  st.um_asks++, uclk++, ufm[set] = way, g->use = t->use = uclk, st.uf_hits++, st.uses++, st.um_hits++, gu_clut_id's own
  note in the load, st.hits++, touch(texture entry), st.pal_uses++, st.pal_hits++, touch(palette entry), the texture's lowest
  alpha worked out again if the palette differs from the last one used with it, t->pid, t->pent, then gl_tex's memo fields.
  Record (per picture, a small table by TEX0 / CLAMP / TEXA in the frame): made when a full look-up took exactly that path:
  set and way, g, t, the two entries, the palette identity gu_clut_id gave (pid_in) and the palette id, the picture's texture
  index and key, and the tokens.
  Reads it relies on, and what guards each:
    a. TEX0, CLAMP, TEXA: compared in full with the record;
    b. the front entry (key, generation), g, t, the entries' ids: TkCache.kgen (new) moves at every look-up that is not on the
       pure path and at every other entry point that changes the cache (tk_get_cl called directly, tk_pool, mode setters); on
       the pure path nothing of these changes. The front's own generation (transfers + local copies + drawn + failures of the
       GS model) is compared with the recorded one;
    c. the palette: gu_clut_id is CALLED for real (as the full step calls it, so its note in the load is the same) and must
       return pid_in without the lost flag; so no token of the CLUT state is needed, and the group's own CLUT load is real;
    d. the picture's texture table: the frame's generation (gl_begin) and tkey[index] == key.
  Replay: the effects listed above, in the same order, by one function in texcache.c next to the full path (tk_keep_again),
  then gl_tex's own answer and memo fields.
  Not kept: look-ups in the check modes of the cache (um 2, uf 2, memo_check), frame-buffer textures, any other path.
  Check (MGS2_NAT_KCHK=1): the full look-up runs instead of the replay and is compared with the record: path pure, same set,
  way, g, t, entries, palette id, index, minalpha; "differing" counted.

Checks common to all parts: twin runs with keep on in every turn, off in every turn, and by turns, in both orders: all 685
state hashes, all 17,664 frame digests and the counters' lines equal between them; the check key on the twin (whole run) and on
the emulator: 0 differing; then the emulator's hashes and pictures against gl34's.

4. LEFT OUT OF gl35, AND WHAT EACH WOULD GIVE
  - batching the groups of one mesh / material into one GE batch: the GE words change (fewer state words, merged vertex runs),
    so it fails the hard gate by definition. Reach: 16.5 percent of groups follow a group with the same material [C]; it
    would save their state set-up on the GE and the GE transform + batch stage (4.2 us a group [C]) for those: about 0.7 us
    a group on average [I: 16.5 percent of 4.2] plus GE time (UNKNOWN).
  - the CLUT load itself (gu_clut_load's kept-load path: about 2.5 of its 3.24 ms a picture [C model, I for the share]):
    a replay needs the state of the 32 CLUT rows and of the load's source blocks equal; the path it would replace is already
    the "load kept for these parameters" one. Expected gain about 1 ms a picture [I]; left for gl36 with the people.
  - the register stage (9.1 us [C]): the three stream descriptors and their owner marks are what lets a later reader of VU
    memory see the group's data; a cheaper form (one descriptor for the interleaved triple) changes the translator's tables
    and counters, so it needs its own design. Not in gl35.
  - the host call's GS decode (xc_gs, 2.08 ms a picture [C model]): depends on TEX0 / ALPHA / PRIM of the material; a keep
    would be per material as K3; small. Not in gl35.
  - the walk and the hand-off (7.6 us [C]): not part of this lever.
Expectation before measuring [I, from the model's per-function costs of gl34's fast arm]: K1 about -3, K2 about -3, K3 about
-4 ms a picture of the ship's 65.7 in the rain wait ME turn: about -10 ms (-15 percent), short of the plan's -35 percent.
The reason is the probe's first line: only the drawn areas are "equal to the group before"; the rest of the fixed work is
real per-material work whose inputs change with five groups of six, and its cost is the length of the code path per group
(123 instruction-cache misses a group [C model]).
