## What this week changed [C]

1. **The PC now draws what your PSP draws.** PPSSPP master with our two patches gives your PSP's shots byte for byte (gl4c, gl4b; `research2_port_plan.md` "Results"). Every picture fix can be checked here pixel for pixel before you run it.
2. **The codec switch-offs were the GH-168 pack, not our code:** 0 stops in 87.9 min of calls without it, 4 in 63.2 min on it. Test runs: the OSTENT or the charger.
3. **Register locals are not worth a rewrite:** register traffic at calls is about 2% of the game code's time (another recompiler gained 13-18% there). The lean generator's step 6 (register arguments) is small for the same reason.
4. **The codec faces:** gl5's P07 converter draws them exactly. Four exact speed-ups (gl6) cut the model's codec step from 132.5 to 115.6 ms (-13%). What is left is mostly instruction-cache misses: each face call runs about 43 KB of code through the 16 KB cache, so the next gains come from batching calls and smaller hot code, not more memos (`codec_speed.md`).
5. **MIST** (an ME library): a candidate for the movies; its kernel-call idea would make waking the ME cheaper (#57, a kernel module: your OK first) (`me_mist_study.md`).

## Later: your decisions

- **Code size:** the full code (4.2 MB, plus 16-22 MB per stage at -O2) does not fit next to the 32 MB of PS2 RAM; every build so far links only the code our routes run. Plan: compiled hot code per stage plus an exact interpreter for the rest (#20, audit H1).
- **Downgrades** where exact work cannot reach the target, each with numbers first and your call: ~~lighter rain~~ (your ask 2026-10-31: the drop sheets near the camera and the foot splash drawn 1 strip in 2, picture only, the game exact; cfg MGS2_GL_RAIN / MGS2_GL_SPLASH; 1/2 or 1/3 your pick; §11.30, shots in SHOTS\rain_and_splash), the trail and blur (OKed if too heavy), GE skinning or 15 fps for the codec faces.
